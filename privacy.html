01Who I am

This website, bajkamalsingh.me (including www.bajkamalsingh.me, together the "site"), is the personal portfolio of Bajkamal Singh, an individual based in Delhi, India ("I", "me").

For personal data I decide how to handle, I am the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the data controller under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"). This policy is written with the DPDP Act and the DPDP Rules, 2025, the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the GDPR in mind. My guiding principle is simple: collect as little as possible.

02Information you send me

The site has no forms, accounts, comment boxes, newsletters or payment features.

The only way I receive personal information directly is if you choose to email me. The "say hi" and "Mail" links just open your own email app. If you write to me, I receive your email address, your name if you include it, and the content of your message and any attachments. I use this only to read and reply to your message and continue the conversation you started. I don't add you to any mailing list.

My email is provided by Google (Gmail), so your message is also processed under Google's Privacy Policy. Please don't email me sensitive information such as passwords, bank or card details, government ID numbers or health information.

03Data processed automatically when you visit

Loading any website means your browser connects to servers, and those servers receive some technical information. The site's own code doesn't collect this information, but the services below process it.

Cloudflare: hosting, security and analytics

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages and served through Cloudflare's network. To deliver pages and protect the site from attacks and abusive bots, Cloudflare processes your IP address, browser and device details (user agent), the page requested, the referring page, the date and time, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. Specifically:

  • Automated browser check. Cloudflare adds a small script to each page that checks whether your browser behaves like a real browser rather than a bot. The result is stored in a security cookie (see section 04).
  • Firewall and bot protection. Cloudflare's firewall, bot protection, browser integrity check and script monitoring may block or challenge suspicious requests.
  • Web Analytics. Cloudflare Web Analytics loads a script from static.cloudflareinsights.com and sends page-view and page-speed measurements back through this site. Cloudflare says this analytics tool doesn't use cookies or local storage, and doesn't fingerprint visitors.
  • Email address protection. Cloudflare hides my email address in the page code from spam bots, and a small script reveals it on screen for you.
  • Network error reports. If the site fails to load properly, for example because of a connection error, your browser may automatically send Cloudflare an error report to help diagnose network problems.

What I can see: my Cloudflare dashboard shows me aggregate statistics, such as visits, pages viewed, referring websites, countries, and browser and device types. Cloudflare's security tools can also show me details of individual requests, including IP addresses, for a sample of traffic and for requests that were blocked or challenged. I use this only to run and protect the site, and I don't try to identify visitors.

Cloudflare's own handling of this data is covered by the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

05What I don't do

  • I don't sell, rent or trade personal data.
  • I don't share personal data for advertising or build profiles of visitors.
  • I don't use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising networks or session-recording tools.
  • I don't intentionally collect sensitive personal data.
  • I don't make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.
  • The site doesn't track you across other websites, so there's nothing extra to switch off if your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal.

06Why data is processed (legal basis)

  • Delivering and securing the site (Cloudflare and the providers above): this is needed to show you the site, keep it working, and prevent attacks and unauthorised access. Under the GDPR, the basis is legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Replying to your emails: you send this information yourself so that I can reply. Under the DPDP Act, this is processing for the purpose you voluntarily provided it for (Section 7(a)), and you can tell me at any time to stop. Under the GDPR, the basis is legitimate interests, or steps you ask for before a contract (Article 6(1)(b) and (f)).

07How long data is kept

  • Emails: I keep them only as long as they're useful for our conversation or opportunity, and I'll delete them if you ask.
  • Cloudflare logs and analytics: kept by Cloudflare for the limited periods set by Cloudflare. I can't change these periods.
  • Font and library providers: kept according to each provider's own policies.

08Security and breaches

The site is served only over HTTPS. Plain HTTP visits are redirected, and HTTP Strict Transport Security is enabled. The site also uses:

  • a Content Security Policy that restricts which outside servers can supply code, styles and fonts;
  • Subresource Integrity checks on most third-party scripts;
  • headers that stop the site being embedded in other websites;
  • Cloudflare's firewall and bot protection.

No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach ever affects information I hold, I'll inform the affected people and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires. If you find a security issue, please report it privately to bajkamalsingh1@gmail.com.

09International transfers

Cloudflare, Google and the other providers above run global networks, so technical data may be processed in countries other than yours, including the United States. They apply their own safeguards to these transfers, as described in their privacy policies.

In practice, the only personal data I hold myself is the emails people send me. To make a request, email bajkamalsingh1@gmail.com. I may need to confirm it's really you before acting on it. If you aren't satisfied with my response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India, or to your local data protection authority if you're in the EU or UK.
